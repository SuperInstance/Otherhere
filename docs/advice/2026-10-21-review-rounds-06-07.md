## Issue #40 is a real race, not noise

The pixel-determinism specs differ by up to ~200/255 across about half of the frame, all below the horizon, between two loads in one run in the dev container. That isn't precision: something in the scene is present in one frame and absent or mid-fade in the other. Bundle bytes match (`verified == ready`).

The likely cause is `settle()` in `walk.spec.ts`: it waits for ready ≥ 7, loading = 0, then 500 ms. At 1–3 fps that is about one frame, and nothing waits for:

- forest chunks to plant;
- grounds to stand (tile fetches to a closed port time out);
- wakes and fades to finish.

CI passes only because its machine renders faster: exactly the pattern `AGENTS.md` warns about.

**Fix:**

- Expose a "display settled" signal: no chunks pending, no wakes running, no tiles in flight.
- Wait for it, then wait for N identical consecutive frames.
- Diff the two PNGs to confirm which layer differed.
- Then restore a byte-exact `equals`, and close P1 #6 (#48) the same way.

# Advice: what to build next, and how

_High-level priorities from the session that built terrain, atmosphere, the forest and the arrival shot. Ordered by player impact per unit of work. Each item says what to watch for, because that is where earlier attempts went wrong._

## 1. Make imagined buildings look like the place

**Problem**: most buildings in Sitka's OpenStreetMap data are `building=yes` with no roof, levels or colour. `building.basic` gives pitched roofs only to explicit house types and makes everything else flat (5:1 against hip). The result is a town of flat white boxes.

**Do**:

- In `building.basic`'s roof fallback (no Fact, no Essence), treat a small `yes` footprint (under ~250 m²) like a house: gable 5, hip 3, shed 1, flat 1. Larger ones keep flat or hip.
- Bump the generator version, re-pin the golden (`scripts/golden.ts`), and look at Sitka from the arrival shot.
- Add a regional prior to Essence, the same way #21 added `biome`. Southeast Alaska: steep gables and sheds, metal roofs (`#5b5f66`, `#7a2e2a`), painted wood siding (barn red, slate blue, cream, forest green, grey). Use it only where no Fact and no Town sample exists, and keep it quantized and hashed (ADR-0014).

**Watch**: imagined colours are deliberately washed toward paper ("soft until sure", `material.ts`). Don't remove that; it's how a player sees what is known. Stronger regional priors only change what the sketch hints at.

## 2. Generated landmarks: the project's showcase

Hand-modelling is what this project exists to avoid. Landmarks are where generated 3D assets matter most: St. Michael's Cathedral (onion dome), the Russian Bishop's House, Castle Hill.

**Do**:

- Add DSL ops for the shapes landmarks need: a lathe or onion dome, a cylindrical drum, a stepped spire, a hipped bell-cast roof.
- A model writes a **generator** (JSON in the existing DSL, never code) from photos and a description. Jev checks it against a checklist (dimensions, number of domes, roof shape), and it enters through a capture-ranked Fact that points at the generator ID. It is versioned like any generator, so Bundles stay content-addressed.
- Keep a human approval step before a generated generator becomes public (`scope`).

**Watch**: invariant 3. The model's output is data that the interpreter reads; it is never executed. Validate it against the DSL schema and a vertex budget before it is stored.

## 3. Weather and time of day

Sitka is wet. Rain streaks (a cheap screen-space or camera-attached particle layer), a lowered cloud ceiling (raise the height-fog density uniform in `atmosphere.ts`), puddle sheen on roads, and dusk with lit windows (an emissive term on the window grid, by time of day). All of it is display-only. Seed it by date if players should share the weather.

## 4. Performance on real devices

Everything so far was judged with software GL in a container.

- Measure frame time on a mid-range phone at the arrival shot and at street level.
- Add automatic quality steps in this order: forest radius and tree budget → terrain `maxTiles` → mist and cloud shadows → pixel ratio.
- `pointer: coarse` already shrinks the forest. A frame-time-driven setting is better than a device guess.

## 5. One forest

See `2026-10-19-forest-architecture.md`. Moving imagined trees to the display layer removes megabytes per Cell, puts every tree on real ground, and gives #21's species silhouettes one home.

## Smaller things

- **Load cost**: `World` loads the walker's Cell ring plus a ring beyond. With terrain, the view reaches kilometres. Distant Cells could load a cheap footprint-only LOD (a generator parameter, still content-addressed) instead of nothing.
- **Sketch Cells**: Cells waiting on Overpass show bare ground at street level. A "loading OpenStreetMap…" cue near the walker would explain the emptiness.
- **Garbage collection**: R2 Bundles are content-addressed and never deleted. Add a periodic sweep of objects not referenced by any `bundles` row in the last N days.
- **`bundleFor` is not single-flight**: two requests for the same Cell build it twice. Memoize the in-flight promise per Cell in the TownDO.

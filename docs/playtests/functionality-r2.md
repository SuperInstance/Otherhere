# Play-test round 2 — functionality (outside-in)

Round OH-A2, 2026-10-20. The instrument: [`apps/client/playtest/probe.mjs`](../../apps/client/playtest/probe.mjs), upgraded per round 1's own notes — HUD panels open before the postcard flow, the sky state (`window.__otherhere.sky`: hour, cloud, wind) recorded in every log row, and four new scenes (`day`, `determinism`, `postcard`, `sketch`) beyond the r1 walk. Sessions: local dev (seeded Zürich, both the vite dev server and a built-client Worker with Overpass closed for a fully offline-deterministic world) and the live deployment. Artifacts in `otherhere-lab/playtest-artifacts/` (`local-zurich-*`, `live-*`, per-run logs in `run-logs/`).

A new instrument this round: the **ambience floor** — two screenshots of the _same_ context moments apart, so the living ocean, foam and cloud drift (which legitimately move under a pinned hour) have a measured pixel cost before any cross-context determinism claim is made.

## What worked

Local and live sessions stayed clean of page errors; bundle hashes verified everywhere (`7/7` on every settled view, `mismatched: 0`). The **director's cut held on live**: two independent contexts at `?t=21.5` read _exactly_ the same sky — hour 21.5, cloud 0.2313, elevation −11.55 to five decimals — and their screenshots differ by 53,730 of 1,024,000 pixels against an ambience floor of 27,898: the pinned sky is identical; the residue is the ocean breathing. Offline (closed-port Worker) the determinism pair differed by only 6,857 pixels — _below_ its own floor. The day-cycle arc ran a full hold-T lap with the status line narrating every leg; the sketch-Cell scene watched a sketch Cell recover on reload with the retry cue in the status line and `cell-asks` empty afterwards.

## Fixes shipped this round (all found from outside, like a player)

1. **The day keeps true speed on slow renderers.** The day clock consumed the walk physics' `dt`, clamped at 0.25 s — on a 0.5 fps software-Gl renderer the day advanced at an eighth of true speed. `skyStep` now gives the sky its own bound (raw `dt`, ceiling 2 s, zero when frozen), so the day turns at true speed however slow the frames arrive.
2. **A retry never blanks its cell.** Reloading a Cell dropped its old group before the new one landed: a sketch Cell's Overpass retry (or a capture sharpening a building) popped the neighbourhood to bare ground for a beat. The old group now stays visible as `stale` until its replacement stands in.
3. **A frozen hour says so.** A pinned `?t=` or postcard view no longer invites "hold T to pass time" — a key that does nothing there. The status line reads "the hour is pinned here" instead.
4. **The weather belongs to the hour.** `cloudCover` was a function of _elapsed watch time_ — two players meeting at the same hour stood under different skies, and a postcard's weather drifted as long as you watched. The noise is now periodic over the day clock (16 coarse systems, 60 fine, both wrapping seamlessly past midnight), so the weather is a property of the hour the way the sun's place is: a postcard or a pinned view keeps the sky it was saved under.
5. **A postcard pins the hour its form was opened**, not the hour the save button lands — the moment you decided to keep is the moment kept.

- `cell-asks` (the retry request log) should record _when_ retries happened against the status line's sketch cue, not only that they ended.

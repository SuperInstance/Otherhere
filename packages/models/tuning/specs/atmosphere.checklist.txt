tone_mapping: The renderer's toneMapping is set to ACESFilmicToneMapping.
dome_backside: The sky dome uses a BackSide material that does not write depth and is not affected by fog.
dome_follows: The sky dome is moved to the camera position in update().
fog_node: The fog is set through scene.fogNode, and scene.fog is set to null.
height_fog: The fog factor grows with distance and is stronger at low world heights than at high ones.
fog_uniforms: Fog distances are uniforms updated in update(), not recompiled materials.
sun_glow: The sky colour brightens toward SUN_DIRECTION.
no_per_frame_alloc: update() does not create new objects, materials or nodes.
dispose_all: dispose() removes the dome and disposes its geometry and material.

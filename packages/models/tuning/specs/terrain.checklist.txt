# Terrain spec, as literal claims about the code
terrarium_decode: Heights are decoded from pixel bytes as R*256 + G + B/256 - 32768.
no_color_conversion: The image is decoded with colorSpaceConversion 'none' and premultiplyAlpha 'none'.
bitmap_closed: Each ImageBitmap's close() method is called after its pixels are read.
concurrency_cap: No more than 6 tile fetches are in flight at the same time.
abort_unneeded: Fetches for tiles that are no longer needed are cancelled with an AbortController.
lru_evict: When there are more tile records than the maximum, the least recently used unused ones are removed.
dispose_geometry: Evicting a tile calls dispose() on its geometry.
dispose_texture: Evicting a tile calls dispose() on its satellite texture and its own material.
heightmap_lru: Fine (zoom 15) heightmaps are kept in their own cache of at most 64 entries.
ancestor_fallback: When a tile's mesh is not loaded yet, a loaded ancestor tile is drawn in its place.
hysteresis: Tiles split and merge at different distance thresholds.
skirts: Tile meshes have skirts: an extra ring of vertices lowered below the tile's edge.
per_vertex_projection: Every vertex position is computed by converting its latitude and longitude with frame.project.
build_budget: At most 4 tile meshes are built per update call.
contours_fwidth: Contour lines use fwidth for anti-aliasing.
flat_fallback: If the first elevation request fails before anything loads, the layer stops making requests and heightAt returns 0.
ready_timeout: ready() resolves after at most about 4 seconds even if tiles have not loaded.
ocean_follows: The ocean plane moves with the camera in x and z.
satellite_lazy: Satellite textures are only requested when the mode is satellite.
no_overlap: A tile drawn in place of its children is never drawn at the same time as any of those children.
mercator_rows: Vertex rows are spaced evenly in Web Mercator y, not evenly in latitude.
pixel_centres: Bilinear sampling treats each pixel's value as belonging at the pixel's centre.
